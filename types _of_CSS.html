Space

It is a near-perfect vacuum filled with low densities of hydrogen and helium plasma, magnetic fields, and cosmic rays.

Sound cannot travel through space because the molecules are too far apart to carry vibrations.

The baseline temperature in deep space is a freezing 2.7 kelvins (−270 °C or −455 °F) due to leftover radiation from the Big Bang.

Outer space officially begins at the Kármán Line, an imaginary boundary located about 62 miles (100 kilometers) above sea level.
